# Community token: exploratory concept

X Market's core idea is a social-commerce experience. A meme token, if independently launched, would be a separate community experiment—not proof that a marketplace exists.

## Possible non-financial community activities

- Participate in public polls about design directions.
- Share concept mockups and community ideas.
- Join community-led creative campaigns.

These are possibilities, not current token-gated features. No contract, blockchain, token symbol, supply, liquidity, staking, yield, governance rights, revenue share, or exchange listing is specified or promised.

## Important disclosures

- No official relationship with X, xAI, Grok, Elon Musk, or Amazon.
- A token does not represent shares, profit participation, or rights to future marketplace features.
- Meme tokens can lose all value and may have no lasting utility.
- No financial returns or price appreciation are promised or implied.
- Any actual token launch would require independent legal, technical, and security review, with verifiable contract information published separately.
