# Vision: commerce that starts with conversation

**Status: speculative concept.**

Traditional marketplaces organize products into catalogs. Social platforms organize attention around people, ideas, and conversations. X Market imagines what a responsible bridge between those worlds could look like.

## Experience principles

1. **Discovery first:** Explore creator-led collections and community-curated products.
2. **Context over clutter:** Compare why a product might be relevant, not just its price.
3. **Trust by design:** Verified merchants, transparent sponsorships, clear returns, dispute resolution, and anti-fraud protections would be necessary before any real launch.
4. **AI as an assistant, not an authority:** Any future AI recommendation system would need disclosures, evaluation, and human override.
5. **Optional crypto:** A community token should never be a requirement to buy essential goods or access basic protections.

## Concept user journey

Discover a product → explore details and creator context → compare options → review seller trust and buyer protections → check out through a compliant payment partner.

**None of the steps involving real accounts, sellers, payments, or AI services are implemented today.**
